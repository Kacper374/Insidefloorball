Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data inside. floorball ("inside.", "we", "us") collects when you visit kacper374.github.io/Insidefloorball, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. inside. is an independent, non-commercial floorball media project — this policy is written with that in mind.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this site is the person running inside. floorball, reachable at kacper.szalanski@onet.pl. If you have any question about your data, that's the address to write to.

2. Data collected through the contact form

When you use the Contact form on this site, the name, email address and message you enter are submitted to and processed by our form provider, Formspree, Inc., which then forwards your message to our inbox by email. We only use this data to read and reply to your message — nothing else.

Formspree may process and store submissions on its own servers as part of providing this service. You can read more in Formspree's own Privacy Policy.

We keep contact form messages only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, and delete them once they're no longer needed — or sooner, if you ask us to.

3. Google Fonts

This site loads the Barlow Condensed and Inter typefaces directly from Google's font servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). To deliver these fonts, Google's servers may receive your device's IP address and standard browser information (such as your browser type and settings). This happens purely so your browser can download the font files — we don't receive or store any of this information ourselves. You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub's servers automatically log basic technical information for every visit (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) to operate and secure the infrastructure. We don't have access to these logs ourselves. You can read more in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Cookies

This site does not currently use cookies, analytics tools, or advertising trackers of its own. If that changes in the future (for example, if we add site analytics or advertising), we'll update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

6. Legal basis for processing

We process contact form data based on your consent, given when you voluntarily submit the form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to messages sent to us.

7. Your rights

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Ask us to restrict or object to how we use your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, just email us at kacper.szalanski@onet.pl.

8. Links to other sites

This site links out to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and our partners' websites. Once you leave inside., this policy no longer applies — those platforms have their own privacy policies, which we'd encourage you to check.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features like site analytics or advertising. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date above.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled? Reach out any time at kacper.szalanski@onet.pl.

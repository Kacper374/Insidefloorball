Northern Conference quarterfinals wrap up as Champions Cup kicks off

Aug 31, 2026

The first round of the 2026/27 Champions Cup quarterfinals was packed with action. Here are six things you can't miss from a week that delivered upsets, heroics and plenty of controversy.

1. Kohonen's 30-metre stunner

The biggest highlight of the whole week belongs to Gabriel Kohonen. With just one second left in the first period, he fired a sweeper from his own half and found the net — a genuinely mind-blowing goal.

2. Nokian stun IBF Falun

Probably the biggest surprise of the round: Nokian eliminated IBF Falun. The decisive moment came with a loss in the opening game on home ice in Falun — not the start to the season Falun had dreamed of. A draw in the rematch at Nokian Arena wasn't enough to turn the tie around.

3. Karjalainen's hero performance

Nokian's run past Falun had a lot to do with goalkeeper Aki Karjalainen, who stepped up as a crucial player in both games. He stopped 36 of 42 shots from Falun and was named MVP of the second game with an impressive 89.47% save rate.

4. A controversial goal for Storvreta

Not every talking point was a highlight. The crucial equalising goal scored by Storvreta's Hampus Nydenfeldt is arguably the biggest lowlight of the week — replays suggest he was standing inside the goal area as he scored, a call the referee missed despite being close to the play. A heartbreaking moment for SPV.

5. Hämäläinen's perfect entrance

Thorengruppen's new signing Suvi Hämäläinen couldn't have asked for a better start. She scored three goals across two games against Finnish opposition Eräviikingit — and all three turned out to be the decisive goals that saved both games for Thoren.

6. FBC Kalmarsund's demolition job

It was never a fair duel: FBC Kalmarsund, packed with young international talent, scored 24 goals across two games against Finnish side Koovee — winning 12–0 in both matches.

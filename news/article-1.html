Northern Conference quarterfinals wrap up as Champions Cup kicks off

Aug 31, 2026

The opening round of the 2026/27 Champions Cup quarterfinals concluded in the Northern Conference, headlined by Nokian KrP's shock elimination of runners-up IBF Falun.

Reigning champions Storvreta IBK also opened their title defence against SPV, as the Northern Conference sides get their campaigns underway before the rest of the field joins the competition.

Attention now turns south, with the Czech and Swiss ties running from late September into October. inside. will keep tracking every tie as the bracket takes shape.
